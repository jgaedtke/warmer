How it works

Each guess is scored by cosine similarity between its word2vec vector (trained on ~100 billion words of Google News) and the secret word's vector, shown ×100. If your guess is among the 1,000 dictionary words closest to the secret, you also see its rank — 1 / 1000 is the very nearest. Similarity reflects how words are used, not strictly what they mean, so opposites and related ideas often score high.
